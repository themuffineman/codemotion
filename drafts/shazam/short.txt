You know when you hold your phone up in a loud bar and Shazam just knows the song? This is how.

First it turns the sound into a picture. Pitch going up, time going across.

Then it throws almost all of it away and keeps only the loudest spots. They look like stars. And the noise in the bar basically can't cover them.

It joins nearby stars into pairs. Two pitches, and the gap between them. That's a fingerprint.

Then it looks those up against every song it knows.

And here's the clever bit. In the right song, every match is off by the exact same amount of time. So when you chart it, the real song is one giant spike.
